Dragon Mode

Dragon Mode is an iPhone app for manually tracking balances, spending, recurring costs, goals and debt in a bright fantasy world. It does not connect to your bank or move money.

Your records, your pace

Map a balance, record a movement or review a statement import. Optional quests, companions and stories reward attention without taking away progress when you miss a day.

Free tools and optional Plus

Recording, statement imports, goals, reminders, stories, earned cosmetics, backups and accessibility features are free. Dragon Mode Plus is a one-time in-app purchase for Flight Path scenarios, 13 Lore calculators and dated Idle Vault interest illustrations. There is no subscription and no paid random reward system.

The App Store displays the localized price before purchase. Availability depends on the released app and your storefront. This page does not announce App Store availability.

Local by design

Your vault stays on your device. Export a backup before deleting the app or changing phones. Estimates use your own assumptions and are not predictions or financial advice.
