BEFORE YOU SET OFF

A few travel notes.

When can I play?

We’re preparing the first release for iPhone and iPad. The App Store link will appear here when it is available.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Campaign, Daily and Free Play work offline once the app is installed. Store purchases, restoring paid items and opening web links need a connection.

Are there ads or subscriptions?

Version 1.0 has no ads or subscriptions. We’re preparing optional permanent cosmetic sets; availability and prices will be shown in the app when they are ready.

Can I move my progress to another device?

You can export and import a save backup from App Info. Progress does not automatically sync between devices. Eligible paid items use Apple’s Restore purchases.

Need a hand?

Visit support for save recovery, globe controls and purchase help.
