Common questions

Does Goblin Mode require an account?

No. Goblin Mode is designed to work locally on your iPhone.

Does the advisor require AI or an API key?

No. The Goblin Advisor is rule-based and works without network services.

How do I remove my data?

Open Settings and use the Data tab to export, import, or reset local state. Deleting the app from your device also removes locally stored app data. Resetting or deleting local data does not remove App Store purchase history.

How do I restore the Cavekeeper Upgrade?

Open Settings, choose About, open Cavekeeper Upgrade, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. The upgrade is a one-time non-consumable, not a subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. Use Apple's purchase support for the Apple Account that made the purchase. If Apple refunds or revokes the upgrade, the cosmetic themes relock while core tools and local progress remain available.

Is Goblin Mode advice?

No. It provides small productivity prompts. It is not a medical, mental health, nutrition, financial, legal, or safety service.
