A few things to know

Clear by nature.

Does it scan my whole Mac, including hidden folders?

DiskTree can index accessible startup volumes and mounted local drives, including hidden files and folders. You can also choose one folder. Exclusions and macOS permissions still apply, and unavailable areas are labelled.

Does it need to keep scanning in the background?

No. Your snapshot is saved locally. Browsing and queries use that snapshot. You can refresh a branch manually or opt into schedules in Growth; scheduled scans run while DiskTree is open.

Are the sizes exact?

DiskTree shows logical file sizes and allocated disk-space estimates. APFS compression, clones, hard links and cloud placeholders can make those differ from physical space you could reclaim. Quick scans intentionally reuse existing sizes; full rescans recheck them.

Can I download it?

DiskTree is currently a personal macOS app in development. There isn’t a public download on this page yet. Get in touch if you’d like to ask about availability.
